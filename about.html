About this project

For the past few years, one of my favorite Christmas traditions has been granting wishes through New York Cares' Winter Wishes program.

This year, by the time I went to sign up, every wish had already been claimed (which is amazing!). So I started looking for other ways to give.

While gathering links to send to a friend who also wanted to participate, I thought: why not put all of these programs in one place so anyone looking for a way to give this Christmas could find them?

And that's how The Christmas Giving List came to be.

I also wanted to be intentional about including programs that serve adults. So much of holiday giving focuses on children, but we don't outgrow the joy of receiving a gift or knowing that someone thought of us.

I hope this website makes it a little easier to give and brings more attention to the incredible organizations working to bring joy into people's lives.

With love,
Crystal

How the list works

  • This website is independent and is not affiliated with any listed organization.
  • Every listing is checked on a regular basis. The main page shows the date it was last checked. Dates and openings change, so confirm with the organization before you buy.
  • This site counts visits and button clicks without cookies or personal data.
